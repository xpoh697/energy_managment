## [v11.1.83] - 2026-04-09
### CRITICAL FIX: TERMINAL SOC RECALIBRATION (MORNING RESERVE FIX)
**PROBLEM**: THE SYSTEM WAS TRAPPING 15-20% EXTRA ENERGY BY ENFORCING A "NOW" SAFETY FLOOR AT THE END OF FUTURE SALES, FORGETTING THAT HOUSE CONSUMPTION HAPPENS OVER TIME. 
**SOLUTION**: REPLACED STATIC SOC FLOOR CHECK WITH A SUNRISE-TARGETED SIMULATION CHECK. THE SYSTEM NOW SALES AS MUCH AS POSSIBLE AS LONG AS THE PROJECTED SOC AT SUNRISE IS >= RESERVE + BUFFER.
**NOTE**: STOP RE-ABUSING THE STATIC FLOOR FOR FUTURE PEAKS. TRUST THE SUNRISE SIMULATION.
